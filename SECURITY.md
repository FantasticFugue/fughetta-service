# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@fughetta.dev**. Please do not open a public issue for a
vulnerability.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit you
tested. A proof of concept helps but is not required.

We acknowledge a report within 3 working days and tell you what we plan to do
about it. Please give us a reasonable time to ship a fix before you disclose
the issue publicly; we will credit you in the release notes unless you prefer
otherwise.

## Scope

This repository (the gateway, source and worker services) and the `fug` CLI.
Reports about third-party dependencies are welcome too, but may be passed on to
the upstream project.

## Other contacts

General questions and account help: **support@fughetta.dev**.
